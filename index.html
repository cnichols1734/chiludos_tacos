Our Story

Where Tradition Meets Bold Flavor

At CHILUDOS Fine Street Tacos, we bring the vibrant spirit of authentic Mexican street food to Mont Belvieu, Texas. Every dish we serve is crafted with passion, using time-honored recipes and the freshest ingredients to deliver flavors that transport you straight to the heart of Mexico.

“We eat here 2-3 times a month. The flavors are incredible and the service is always top-notch.”

From our signature birria tacos dripping with rich, savory consommé, to our creative Tacos Pulpo and the beloved mango jalapeño margarita, every item on our menu tells a story of culinary tradition reimagined with bold, modern flair.

Our Menu

Signature Dishes & Favorites

From classic street tacos to creative specialties, every dish is crafted with passion and the finest ingredients.

Best Seller

Birria Tacos

Slow-braised beef in rich chili consommé, topped with fresh onion, cilantro, and served with a side of savory broth for dipping.

Unique

Tacos Pulpo

Tender grilled octopus tacos with our house-made chimichurri, pickled red onion, and a squeeze of fresh lime on handmade tortillas.

Classic Street Tacos

Your choice of perfectly seasoned al pastor, carne asada, carnitas, or pollo on fresh corn tortillas with cilantro and onion.

Carne Asada Plate

Juicy grilled steak served with rice, refried beans, fresh pico de gallo, guacamole, and warm handmade tortillas.

Morning

Breakfast Tacos

Start your morning right with fluffy scrambled eggs, your choice of bacon, chorizo, or barbacoa on fresh flour tortillas.

Fan Favorite

Mango Jalapeño Margarita

The perfect balance of sweet mango and spicy jalapeño with premium tequila. Our most requested drink — rated 10/10 by customers!

Pork Cracklins Guacamole

Fresh hand-mashed guacamole served with crispy pork cracklins for the ultimate appetizer experience. A customer favorite!

Fideo Soup

Traditional Mexican noodle soup simmered in a rich tomato broth with tender chicken. The ultimate comfort food, just like abuela used to make.

Flan

Silky smooth caramel custard, made fresh daily using our traditional family recipe. The perfect sweet ending to your meal.
